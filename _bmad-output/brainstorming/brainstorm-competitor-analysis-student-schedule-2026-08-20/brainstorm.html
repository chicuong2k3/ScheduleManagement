6 Learn vs. Don't Copy

✓ What to LEARN

  • Weeklie Offline, no account, one-time payment, no subscription creep
  • Student Trove Local-first encrypted storage; offline OCR import; accessibility
  • Classcard Flat transparent pricing; family billing; lead→enrolled in one action
  • TutorBird Same-day setup speed; simple solo invoicing; free tier
  • VUS × Zalo Mini App + ZNS: 4× lead quality, +26pp interaction, −30% data processing
  • VN mobile entrants Zalo Mini App for teachers; SePay/VietQR; QR attendance; 30-day trials
  • MoMo Payroll API + VietQR T+0 payouts; VietQR-embedded PDF invoices
  • Face-ID (Daytot/CenterUp) Bring school-grade face-ID to the tutoring tier (sub-2s)
  • Power Planner GPA/goal calculator as engagement hook

✕ What NOT to copy

  • MyStudyLife Data-destroying redesigns; broken AI; paywall creep; forced re-login; bloat
  • iStudiez Pro Abandonment; sync blocking the UI; no offline write-conflict handling
  • TutorCruncher %-of-revenue fees; non-intuitive UX; English-only; slow bug fixes
  • Teachworks "Mobile app not planned"; 60+ add-on paralysis; per-lesson pricing surprises
  • Classcard $249/mo extra for branded apps (mobile = upsell)
  • Zalo-as-notification-only Broadcast channel instead of primary parent UX
  • PC-first admin Desktop-bound management with limited mobile admin
  • Enterprise pricing + sales-led onboarding 45M VND iLeader; contact-only MISA
  • Web-only everything The global B2B tier's cardinal sin
  • Per-student / per-lesson / %-of-revenue pricing All punish growth; opacity breeds churn

🎯 The Play

One engine. Two roles. Three windows. Built in the order that compounds.

A

Zalo-native tutor triangle

VN-first. Parent on Zalo Mini App, tutor in-app, student in-app — one thread per student. Beats Daytot/CenterPro/CenterUp which treat Zalo as a notification channel.

B

Solo tutor on rails

Global expansion arc. 10-minute setup, auto-invoicing, WhatsApp Business backbone — versus web-only Zutor/Tutorbase/TutorSolo.

C

Offline-first planner

Emerging markets. Reliable on intermittent 3G, never loses data — versus broken MyStudyLife and defunct iStudiez. Zegju proved it: offline-first enrolled half of Ethiopia's freshmen.
